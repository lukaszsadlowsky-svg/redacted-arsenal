About Redacted Arsenal

Redacted Arsenal is an independent project dedicated to experimental weapons, prototype military vehicles and overlooked chapters of technological history.

Each episode examines not only what was built, but why it was created, what problem it attempted to solve and why it ultimately disappeared.

Behind the Arsenal

I’m Lucas, the creator behind Redacted Arsenal. I’ve always been fascinated by military history, engineering and experimental machines that never reached mass production.

Redacted Arsenal is my way of documenting these overlooked stories and bringing them to life through research, visual storytelling and downloadable dossiers.
